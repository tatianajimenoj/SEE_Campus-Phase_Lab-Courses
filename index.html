Module Workshop

Hands-on Module Construction & Characterization

PV Module Pilot Line Workshop

Wednesday, September 30, 2026
9:00 AM – 2:00 PM (CEST) · ~4 hours 30 minutes (excl. break)
Munzinger Str. 10, 791111, Freiburg

Learning Objectives

  1. 1

    Describe the main steps for producing a PV module

  2. 2

    Attach cross connectors to solar cell strings, and assess the quality of the connection

  3. 3

    Explain the encapsulation procedure, including the selection and application of encapsulant materials (EVA, backsheet, glass), and identify correct lamination parameters.

  4. 4

    Analyze I-V curve measurements under standard test conditions and interpret key electrical parameters (Voc, Isc, Fill Factor, Pmax) to assess module performance and identify defects.

  5. 5

    Conduct visual inspection and evaluate electroluminescence (EL) tests to identify common defects such as cracks, bubbles, delamination, and dead cells, and evaluate module quality against acceptance criteria.

  6. 6

    Describe the complete production workflow from stringing to final testing, and relate how each production stage impacts overall module quality and compliance with industry standards.

Schedule

9:00 AM – 9:15 AM Security & Safety Briefing 15 min
11:30 AM – 12:00 PM Break 30 min

Planned Stations / Topics

Tap a station to expand its full procedure.

Activities

  • Conduct a short facility tour to observe main equipment in a production line
  • Discuss quality checkpoints at each step and process parameters

Procedure

  1. 1Demonstration (15 min): Instructor shows stringer and correct tabbing and busbar techniques.
  2. 2Guided practice (30 min): Students attach cross connectors to strings of solar cells.
  3. 3Quality check (10 min): Students measure Voc with multimeter to verify electrical continuity
  4. 4Discussion (5 min): Common errors and troubleshooting tips

Procedure

  1. 1Explanation (15 min): Discuss material selection, layup sequence, and lamination parameters (temperature, vacuum, pressure, time)
  2. 2Layup practice (20 min): Students arrange layers in correct order
  3. 3Lamination (15 min): Run layup through lamination cycle (or observe demo)
  4. 4Post-lamination inspection (10 min): Examine edge seal, clarity, and uniformity

Procedure

  1. 1Introduction (10 min): Explain STC conditions, I-V curve fundamentals, and key parameters
  2. 2Measurement demonstration (5 min): Show proper module placement and measurement setup
  3. 3Hands-on testing (20 min): Each group tests 2–3 modules and records Voc, Isc, FF, Pmax
  4. 4Data analysis (10 min): Compare results, identify underperforming modules, discuss causes

Procedure

  1. 1Visual inspection demo (10 min): Show inspection criteria, lighting, and documentation
  2. 2Visual inspection practice (10 min): Students inspect samples and record defects
  3. 3EL testing demonstration (5 min): Explain principle, show operation, interpret images
  4. 4EL testing practice (15 min): Students image modules, identify hidden defects (cracks, inactive cells)
  5. 5Comparison & discussion (5 min): Correlate visual findings with EL images

Activities

  • Wrap up discussion and reflection
  • Reflection
  • Q&A: Open forum for remaining questions
